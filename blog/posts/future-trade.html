Future Trade


Future — something that will exist or happen at a later time.

Market — a meeting together of people with the purpose of trade.

Purpose — To negotiate a deal (trade)

In this market what is something?

An Agreement for a transaction to take place at a later time in the future. 

Agreement — An agreement to buy/sell some commodity on a given date for a given price. 

Current Date — Today; Buyer — 100 Kg Rice [$100]; Seller — Produce 100 kG rice. The transaction will be done on next month.

Why agree to buy something in the future when you can simply buy it right now?

 or

Why agree to sell something in the future when you can simply sell it right now?

Make something from uncertainty to certainty in future.

 buyer’s perspective → 

A huge focus for the buyer to make money is to keep their cost of production as controlled and predictable as possible. 

The buyer thinks an XRP coin is $3 now. It might rise above $5 in the future due to some beliefs. Consecutnety he aims to lock the XRP in $3. 

then in future for some reason, if the XRP reaches $5 the buyer will buy those coins for $3 instead of $5 because he agreed on $3.

From the buyer's perspective, when this deal happens, yes good job. we predict the future.

Seller’s perspective → 

A huge focus for the seller to make money is to sell their product as high as the market buyers are willing to pay.

In the future, Seller might think XRP may drop to $2. The seller agrees that he will sell the XRP at $2 in the future. but in the future, it may drop to $1.5 however seller already made an agreement and hence he will sell $2. 

The strategies of hedging are the pillars of the futures market. This is also known as a physical settlement. 

  1. Commodity Futures — Items that can be touched physically.
  2. Financial Futures — which are intangible

How do Traders and investors make money in future trading?

As I said XRP is $3 and it is called as spot price / current price.

As time moves forward, the price of XRP will change. Sometimes it would rise to $4 or it will go down to $2. 

when the spot prices change, the value of the contract changes. The change in the value of the contract itself is what allows trades to make/lose money from the contract. 

The act of giving or taking contracts in return for money.

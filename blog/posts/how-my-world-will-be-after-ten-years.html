How My World will be After Ten Years

How can we make this world a better place for everyone? Yeah Surely, It is a tough thing but we need to do. The thing this how can we do these normal human things over and over. Its sustainable development. This sustainable development is depended on the social process, economical development and climate and environment. First, We need to take care of nature. We have many natural ecosystems that must be in balance in order for us to live here.

Sustainable development goals(SDGs) are goals which we need to achieve to make this world better for everyone. Wait what is SDGs?
The Sustainable Development Goals are a collection of 17 global goals designed to be a “blueprint to achieve a better and more sustainable future for all”. Above definition was taken from the Wikipedia. United Nations General Assembly was set following goals as SDGs and they have planned these goals will be achieved before 2030.

Eight goals of MDGs

  1. To eradicate extreme poverty and hunger
  2. To achieve universal primary education
  3. To promote gender equality and empower women
  4. To reduce child mortality
  5. To improve maternal health
  6. To combat HIV/AIDS, malaria and other diseases
  7. To ensure environmental sustainability
  8. To develop a global partnership for development

If you look at all the goals of MDGs are defined for developing country, not for a developed country. This is the reason why SDGs comes to the picture. This is applicable for universal and all countries.

17 Goals of SDGs

  1. No Poverty → End poverty in all its forms everywhere
  2. Zero hunger → Achieve food security and improved nutrition and promote sustainable agriculture
  3. Good health and well -being → Ensure health lives promote well being for all at all ages
  4. Quality Education → Ensure inclusive and quality education for all and promote lifelong learning
  5. Gender equality → Achieve gender quality and empower women and girls
  6. Clean water and sanitation → Ensure the access to water and sanitation for all
  7. Affordable and clean energy → Ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern energy for all
  8. Decent work and economic growth →Promote inclusive and sustainable economic growth, employment and decent work for all
  9. Industry, innovation and infrastructure → Build resilient fracture, promote sustainable industrialization and foster innovation
  10. Reduced inequalities → Reduce inequality and among countries.
  11. Sustainable cities and communities → Make cities inclusive safe resilient and sustainable.
  12. Responsible consumption and production → Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns
  13. Climate Action →Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts
  14. Life below water → Conserve and sustainably use the oceans, seas and marine resources
  15. Life on land → sustainably manage forests, combat desertification, halt and reverse land degradation, halt biodiversity loss.
  16. Peace, justice and strong institutions → Promote just, peaceful and inclusive societies
  17. Partnerships for the goals → Revitalize the global partnership for sustainable development

This SDGs can be applied for each country, cities, schools, organisations and all. That's why we called this Universality. All SDGs are interconnected, We can’t achieve a particular goal. We need to work on all goals rather than focussing one goal. This is why we say SDGs has integrity.

SDGs gives a better way to express how the world should be in after ten years.

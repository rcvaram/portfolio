What is a conflict of interest

When a person has a competing interest which could be in conflict with each other. For example, A teacher who sets a paper for a class where one of the students is his/her son. Existing conflict of request is not bad and it is common in a connected world. So where the problem comes? When you have a conflict of interest, and you are ignoring the conflict of interest or you are waiting until it is exposed by other channels.

The best way to solve the conflict of interest is to notice your conflicts to the suitable authorities and that one removes yourself from the conflict.

For example, you are one of the members of a selection committee of your company and your son will be an interviewee. In that time, telling this conflict your company and removing yourself from the conflict is the best way to avoid the conflict of interest.

Privacy

Privacy is the notation of access. when someone says it’s my bag it means they don’t allow anyone to access their bag. they maintain the bag privacy and they will not give permission to people. Privacy can differ from person to person. More than that, privacy is a human right.

Your individual growth and responsibility, freedom to be yourself, intellectual and spiritual growth will be affected due to privacy. This privacy may be used to cover for illegal or anti-social activities, the people who has problems such as psychiatric issues, dysfunctional issues will not be got help from people since they did not talk with others. The privacy may trigger to loneliness.

How this privacy is violated?

public records such as birth certificates, marriage certificates, NIC information, motor vehicle records, criminal records and more will affect our privacy and more than that when these records are computerized, the records are easier to access.

When My Heart Was Confused, This Lesson Changed Everything

Last February, I traveled with my family to Thiruvennamalai, seeking something spiritual.

There was a wise man. His advice was very interesting and useful. 
That’s why many people would search and come to hear him speak. 
One day, I too went looking for him.

My heart had so much pain and confusion. So the moment I saw the wise man, I told him everything in my heart. 
I told the wise man,

“Sir, in my heart, negative thoughts come again and again… and when they come, I get scared… 
scared that maybe each one of them will happen.
What can I even say…

You know,
if someone says fire, it doesn’t burn,
if someone says sweet, it doesn’t even taste sweet on the tongue.

But these negative thoughts, even if they come just a little,
they bring big confusion and shake my heart badly.

How do I come out of this?” I asked.

The wise man smiled and told me to turn around and look back. I turned and looked behind me.

There was a big cornfield. As far as the eye could see, only corn.
But in that cornfield, there stood a scarecrow.

I kept staring at it. I didn’t get anything from it.

Then the wise man asked, “Does that scarecrow have life?”
 I said, “No.”

The wise man asked again, “Can that scarecrow talk? Can it walk from there to here?”
I said, “No way, definitely not.”

“If that scarecrow can’t talk, can’t walk, has no life… then why do all the birds get scared when they see it?” he asked.

I said, “Only we humans with 6 sense know that the scarecrow has no life and can’t move or speak. Poor birds… they only have 5 sense, right? They don’t know that. That’s why they get scared and fly away.”

The wise man then said,
“Our heart is just like that.

If we don’t train it right,
like the bird that gets scared of a lifeless scarecrow,
our heart also gets scared seeing these negative thoughts
without even knowing they are just fake.

Try training your heart a little.
These negative thoughts… they’re just fake.
They don’t have life. They don’t have feelings.
They’re not going to win in your life.

You have so many good thoughts inside you, better than all this.
Only those good thoughts are going to come true.
Only good things are going to happen in your life.

Say that to your heart.
So next time when negative thoughts come,
your heart won’t get confused,
won’t get scared anymore,” he said.

Yes… that’s true.

In this time where everywhere you turn you hear some bad news,
how can the heart not get negative thoughts?
When those thoughts come, naturally it brings fear and mess, right? That’s just how it works.

So we can’t stop those thoughts from coming.
But even if they come,
we can train our heart not to sit and think about it.
We can train it to think of good things instead.

That’s what I understood. Hope you will also understand…

Is Feminism a scam?

Feminism was a scam to push women into the workforce, so governments could tax both parents and control children through schools.

Today Morning, I watched a reel that has 500K+ views and 27K likes. The message is kinda bold and make you rethink on feminism.

The real claimed Feminism was a planned system to control families, increase taxes, and influnce children.

https://www.instagram.com/reels/DWJ17R-kWrn/

Read it here. If you are blocked by Medium paywall.

The reel says a clear story:

  • Before feminism, one income could support a whole family
  • Women mostly stayed home and raised children
  • Feminism pushed women into the workforce

Which makes

  • Powerful groups like the Rockefeller Foundation, Ford Foundation, and even the CIA supported feminism.
  • The aim was to double the tax base by making both parents work.

And then comes the “most disturbing facts” of the reel:

  • If both parents work, children spend more time in school
  • Schools then become responsible for shaping children
  • The same powerful groups influence what is taught
Feminism created a system where both parents must work, earn less individually, and give up control of raising their children.

Is this reel information is real?

Let’s break it down along with questions and answers.

1. Was there a “one income golden age”?

Yes, in some countries and time periods ( post–World War II), a single income could support a middle-class family.
But
This was not true for everyone

Many families (especially lower-income) needed multiple earners

Women have always worked in different forms, just not a always in formal jobs

historical data shows:

  1. In the early 1900s, around 20% of women were already working
  2. Only about 5% of married women worked for wages in the 1930s
  3. By 1970, about 40% of married women were working

The “perfect past” described in the reel is selective, not universal.

2. Was feminism created to increase taxes?

More workers → more taxes → feminism was created for taxes

There is no solid historical proof that

  • Governments or elites created feminism as a tax strategy
  • The movement was designed to push women into jobs

Feminist movements began with goals like:

  • Voting rights
  • Education access
  • Legal equality

3. What about Rockefeller, Ford, and the CIA?

Ofcourse Yes,

  • Large foundations funded education and social programs
  • Women’s studies programs received funding in the 20th century

But

  • Funding education is not the same as creating a global movement
  • Feminism existed before and outside these institutions
  • There is no strong evidence of a coordinated plan to control society through feminism
This is a classic pattern: real names + missing context = convincing story.

4.Did more workers reduce wages?

more workers → lower wages.

But it’s not true.

economic data shows:

  1. Women’s workforce participation grew from ~37% in 1960 to ~60% by 2000
  2. This helped increase economic output and living standards

When more people work

  • The economy grows
  • New industries appear
  • Total production increases

Wage stagnation today is real, but it is linked to

  • Globalization
  • Automation
  • Policy decisions
  • Inequality

We can’t simply say its only becoz of women joined the workforce.

5. Are schools raising your kids?

The reel say that once both parents work, schools take over completely.

Actually,

  • Public schooling existed long before modern feminism
  • Curricula are influenced by governments, local communities, educators, and public debate and not by a single group.

historically,

  • Public schooling existed long before modern feminism
    By 1918, compulsory education laws existed across the United States
  • Education systems expanded mainly to improve literacy and economic development

Yes, Schools influence children however they are not a single, centralized system controlled by one group.

6. Why does this message feel true?

Even if the explanation is flawed, the feeling behind it is real.

Today:

  • Many families need two incomes
  • Costs are high
  • Time with children feels limited

This reel connects quickly because it gives a simple explanation for the worries we worried.

Actually,

The reel is effective because it turns a complex problem into a simple story:

  • One cause (feminism)
  • One group (powerful elites)
  • One outcome (control and profit)

But when you examine it closely, the evidence doesn’t support that conclusion.

Reality is,

  • Multiple causes
  • Gradual changes
  • Trade-offs

Feminism did not create all modern problems. And it was not a secret plan to control families.

It was one of many forces that changed how society works. Analysing and Understanding that difference matters when something goes viral.

Treated glare as a Noise in Images

Basically, The nature colour of the image (Diffuse colour component of the image) changes smoothly in the entire image so when glare or specular highlight is in the image then that can be treated as noise.

How can we eliminate noise in images? Using Low pass filters, we can reduce the noise in the images. For example, we can use a bilateral filter that is normally used to smoothing and edge-preserving to remove the glare noise from the image.

We need to define a function that can calculate the dichromatic or natural colour of the specular pixel. Hereafter I will mention the natural colour as dichromatic colour.

As usual, we take the original colour of the specular pixel and the maximum fraction of the diffuse colour component.

Here, we may be confused about how can we calculate the maximum fraction of the diffuse colour component of that pixel. One method is using neighbourhoods which iteratively shifts chromaticity values towards those of the neighbouring pixel having the maximum chromaticity in the neighbourhood was proposed. Another method is, by directly applying a low-pass filter to the maximum fraction of the colour components of the original image, such that the maximum diffuse chromaticity values can be propagated from the diffuse pixels to the specular pixels.

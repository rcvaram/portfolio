Gradient descent is the algorithm to reach the best fit line for given training data set with small iterations

How gradient descent is working()?
 for that, We have to plot the m & b against the cost function(MSE) where is the local mimimum is there, Intially we take m,b as zero and we calculate the cost function we will changne the m & b and we will calculate the cost function again and we will get a local minimum an here we will take the tiny step to reduce the cost function. How we will take these tiny steps? one approach is taking fixed-size steps, we may miss the global minimum and other one is first we will take large tiny steps and we will reduce the size of step to global minimum. For this approach, we have to calculate the slop and get the direction

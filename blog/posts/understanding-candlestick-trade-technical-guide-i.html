Once you have found a crypto coin on the market, you need to know how to analyse the chart or the prize of the coin over time. This is called Technical Analysis.

I prefer to analyse using candlestick charts. Since the candlestick provides more information about the price action in the specific time frame.

This chart is every 5 minutes candlestick chart. What that means → each candlestick represents 5 minutes of the market trend.

Let's understand the green candlestick

Let's talk about this green candlestick [07:02 30 - 07:07 30]

Open / Start — It is the start / open price for the time frame basically, the SOL coin value of 07:02:30 was $246.90

End / Close — It is the end / close price for the time frame basically, the SOL coin value of 07:07:30 was $247.80

Low — The wick we had below open is called Low that is the minimum price that $SOL went during this period (07:02:30–07:07:30) and it was $246.31

High — The wick we had above close is called High that is the maximum price that $SOL went during this period (07:02:30–07:07:30) and it was $247.89

Quick view on CandleStick

  • The body of the candle is rectangular which shows the open and close points.
  • The tiny lines are called shadows or candle wicks which show the high and low prices of the coin in that time frame.
  • The green candle opens up at the bottom and closes at the top and for the red candle, it is vice versa. Hope this makes sense because the green candle shows the market is moving high and the red candle shows the market is going down.

What does candle's body tell us?

  • The size of the candle's body communicates the sentiment. It is proportional to sentiment. Like if we have a large body then the sentiment is also large.
  • Sentiment means whether the market falls with buyers/sellers
  • A large-sized green candlestick means the market has strong buyers like it the open price is $2 and the close price is $100 there is a strong sentiment for the coin for buyers. buyers or demand is significantly increased for the coin.
  • A small-sized green candlestick means the market has weak buyers like its open price is $2 and close price is $3. simply means, the market is green however the demand is low the price has not significantly increased during the time frame.

What do candle’s wicks tell us?

Candle wicks communicate a battle
  • Upper candle wicks are bearish because they show that sellers were stronger and pulled the price back
  • Here if you see Buyers try to buy for around $272.03 however sellers are increased and when the market close price is around 270.59. If you see here, the wick length is ~$2 and the body length is $0.5. Here the sellers are really strong to make the price not move forward.

You can see the same thing here as well.

If you see a high big wick on top/upper shadow it means sellers are strong in the battle to reduce the price.
  • Lower candle wicks are bullish because they show buyers were strong and pulled the price back up.
  • Here if you see sellers try to sell for around $263.86 however buyers are increased and when the market close price is around $265.29. If you see here, the wick length is ~$1.41 and the body length is $0.28. Here the buyers are really strong to make the price not go down.

You can see the same thing here as well.

If you see a low big wick on down /lower shadow it means buyers are strong in the battle to increase the price.

Let's make a summary of candlestick

The candlestick has four parts, OPEN, CLOSE, HIGH and LOW
The candle body communicates sentiment. If the body is large then strong sentiment. Green means buyer's side and red means seller's side.
The candle wicks communicate a battle .. Upper candle wicks are bearish and Lower candle wicks are bullish

I Studied Naval Ravikant’s Mental Models — Here’s What Actually Matters (Part 3)

Part 3 of a series

In Part 1, we covered wealth.
In Part 2, we covered thinking.

Now we move to something deeper.
How to live.

Most people try to improve their life from the outside. Very few fix what’s inside. That difference changes everything.

Happiness Model

Happiness is the default state.

You don’t gain happiness.
You remove what blocks it.

  • Stress
  • Fear
  • Overthinking.
  • Ask:
“What is preventing my happiness?”

Desire Model

Desire is a contract you make with yourself
to be unhappy until you get something.

Before wanting something, ask:

Do I need it for its use, or just to remove a feeling?

Fewer desires, more peace.

Health Model

Health is more important than wealth.

Focus on:

  • Sleep
  • Food
  • Mental peace

Exercise alone is not enough.

If your health fails, everything else follows.

Compounding

Everything in life compounds:

  1. Effort
  2. Skills
  3. Money
  4. Relationships

At first, results are slow. Later, they grow exponentially.

Consistency beats intensity.

Life Habits

Small habits shape your life.

  1. BOOKS: Reading improves thinking
  2. READ WHAT YOU LIKE: Stay consistent
  3. AVOID NEWS: Reduce noise
  4. SOLITUDE: If you cannot sit alone, your mind is overloaded
  5. NO PHONE RULE: Protect your mornings
  6. FORGIVENESS: Let go and move forward
  7. ABUNDANCE MINDSET: There is enough for everyone
  8. VECTOR THINKING: Focus on direction, not speed
  9. ZERO-SUM GAMES: Avoid win-lose situations

Life has no fixed meaning. It is a blank page.

You decide what to write, and that becomes your life.

This is Part 3 of the series. If you haven’t read Part 1 and Part 2, start there to understand the full picture.

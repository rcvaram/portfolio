Don’t Love anyone Too much

Love is amazing, right? It makes us feel all happy and fuzzy with our families, BFFs, and maybe even a special someone. But here’s the thing: Love is awesome, but you gotta keep it balanced!

Imagine you love KFC Chiken so much you order five extra KFC chiken buckets at once. That might seem great at first, but you’ll probably feel awful later, right? The same goes for love in relationships. If you love someone SO much you forget about yourself or let them walk all over you, it can actually hurt both of you.

Love is incredible, but you gotta use it wisely

You gotta take care of YOU first!

Think of it like putting on an airplane oxygen mask first. ✈️ You can’t help others if you’re passed out! Make sure you’re happy and healthy so you can share that awesomeness with others. ✨

Everyone needs some “me time” sometimes.

Don’t be that person who texts their crush every 5 seconds. Give each other space to do your own thing and be yourselves. This actually makes the relationship stronger because you have more interesting things to talk about! ️

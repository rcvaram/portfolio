2. Naan unaki olunga sonnaan naan athila thaan nikiran endu nee omm koncham wait pannungo endu sonnaai... then thirumpa call panni sonnaai... naanga ingaala vanthidam, appa naan sonnan ingala ello wait panna sonnaai endu athuku nee aah naan ingaala vanthidan endu thirumpa solraai... enn angaala varela endu reason sollamaa
3. Naan work time la unakkaaka thaane bike la vanthan, unakaaka thaane dress eduthan unakaaka thaane vanthu nindan unada thatabum endu thaane avolo efforts podan 15 minutes la vanthan and, thirumpa 10 to 15 minutes la podan... - nee simple message la kekira unnai vara sonnaanaa endu

5. Sari thirumpa phone edukiran again silenece la

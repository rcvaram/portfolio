Price Action & Volume Mastery

These are brief crypto investment self notes I created with ChatGPT, and this article is open to everyone. 

1. Market Phases

Crypto markets cycle through 4 key phases:

  1. Accumulation — Sideways price action after a downtrend, low volume.
  2. Markup — Breakout with increasing volume = trend begins.
  3. Distribution — Sideways after an uptrend, often with traps and fakeouts.
  4. Markdown — Breakdown on volume = trend reversal down.
🔍 Recognizing the phase you’re in gives context to all price and volume behavior.

Key Price Action Concepts

  • Higher Highs & Higher Lows (HH/HL) = Uptrend.
  • Lower Highs & Lower Lows (LH/LL) = Downtrend.
  • Doji / Pin Bars / Engulfing Candles = reversal or indecision signals.
  • Breakout + Retest = Reliable entry pattern if volume supports it.
📌 Focus less on the candle name, more on the story behind it. Who’s in control — buyers or sellers?

4. Trap Signals to Watch

  • Fakeouts: Price breaks a key level, then reverses. Often on low volume.
  • Stop Hunts: Sudden spikes to sweep liquidity before reversing. Use volume and structure to filter noise.

⏳ 5. Context + Confluence = Smart Trading

  • Combine price structure, volume behavior, and key levels (support/resistance).
  • Look for confluence: e.g., bullish engulfing candle + breakout above resistance + strong volume.

🔁 Quick Framework:

To evaluate any setup:

  1. Is the price in a clear trend or range?
  2. Are buyers or sellers showing control via candles?
  3. Is volume confirming or diverging from price?
  4. Are we near a key level (support/resistance/EMA)?
  5. Is there a pattern forming? (Optional bonus)

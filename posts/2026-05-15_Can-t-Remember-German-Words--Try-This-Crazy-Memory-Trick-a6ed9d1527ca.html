Can’t Remember German Words? Try This Crazy Memory Trick

Can’t remember a German word even after writing it 50 times?

Let’s fix that with a powerful memory technique that makes vocabulary stick in the fastest way possible.

Idea → We connect every German word with something familiar in English. Basically, we create a funny mental image or memory hook for each German word.

These ideas may be stupid. but just think, we just need to remember words that’s the only goal we have here.

The human brain always remember weird, emotional, exaggerated, visual, and funny things much faster than boring repetition.

Technique

Instead of memorizing: → gross = big

we create a story around it. Think about your workplace. Your gross salary is always bigger than your net salary, right?
Now your brain connects:

gross → big

So the next time someone asks you what “gross” means in German, your brain immediately jumps to “gross salary,” which means a bigger salary.

That is how memory mapping works.

From now on, I’ll give you a hook for every word so you can remember it for a very long time. I personally used this technique to memorize my A1 vocabulary.

If this style helps you, drop a comment. I’ll continue sharing more words and memory tricks.

Also remember this rule:

  • Funny
  • Emotional
  • Visual
  • Exaggerated
  • Sound-related
  • Slightly stupid

1. Gross — Big

Just remember salary. A gross salary is bigger than the net salary.
So: gross → big

2. Klein — Small

Klein sounds a little like coin. Coins are small.
So: klein → small

3. alt — old

No fancy trick needed. It already sounds close to “old.”

4. jung — young

Again, the sound is already close .jung → young

5. allein — alone

In German, ein means one. If someone is single or just one person, they are alone.
So: all + ein = only one = alone

6.nett — nice

Think about the internet. Internet people act like nice noh..
So: nett → nice

7. neu — new

This already sounds very close to “new.”

8. toll — great / superb

When something is amazing, we say it’s good “at all levels.” “Toll” sounds energetic and powerful.
So:toll → great

9. arm — poor

A poor person stretches out their arm asking for money.
So: arm → poor

10. kaputt — broken

This already exists in English slang. “My laptop is kaput.”
So: kaputt → broken

11. krank — sick/ill

When people are sick, they become cranky.
So: krank → cranky → sick

12. gesund — healthy

Think: “good sound body.” “Gesund” sounds fresh and healthy.
So: gesund → healthy

These are only the first 12 words from my personal German memory-correlation method.

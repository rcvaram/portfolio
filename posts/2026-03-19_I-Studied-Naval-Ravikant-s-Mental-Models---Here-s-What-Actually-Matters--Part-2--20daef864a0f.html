I Studied Naval Ravikant’s Mental Models — Here’s What Actually Matters (Part 2)

Most people try to work harder. Very few try to think better.
That difference changes everything.

In Part 1, we understood the foundation — wealth, specific knowledge, and accountability. Now we move to something deeper.

Leverage

Leverage means getting more output from the same input.

Types of leverage:

1. People — Hard to manage and creates dependency

2. Capital — Money generates more money

3. Code and Media — Best form of leverage

Examples of code/media:

  • Videos
  • Books
  • Software
  • AI

You create once and distribute infinitely.

First Princinple of THINKING

Do not think based on examples.

Think from fundamentals.

Ask: “What is the basic truth behind this?”

Keep going deeper until no further explanation exists.

Be impatient in action, but patient in results.

Clear Thinking

Sit quietly and think slowly.

It may not give correct answers immediately, but it gives clarity.

It shows what you understand 
and what you don’t.

Inversion Thinking

Instead of asking “How to succeed?”, 
ask “How can I fail?”

Avoid those actions and, 
success becomes easier.

Reality Thinking

Do not believe things just because they feel good.

Focus on facts, logic, and truth.

Truth may be uncomfortable, 
but it is still truth.

Long-Term Thinking

Ask: “Can I do this for a long time?”

This applies to:

  • Diet
  • Work
  • Relationships
  • Business

Long-term decisions are usually the best decisions.

Most people try to optimize actions. Naval focuses on optimizing thinking.

The difference is not effort — it’s clarity.

Universal Design Principles


Equitable use

Equitable means not about equal, it means considering all facts that the user has to experience in the context. It talks about the fair usage of the system. For example, One user has good bandwidth and another user has poor bandwidth. If you design carefully the system, then both users may get the fair chance of accessing your system. Simply Equitable is not simply talking about equal like men and women rights. This is about users. The users don’t feel bad because of the context. Your design accommodates all to use your system in a fair manner.

Flexibility in use

Users should feel flexible when they use your system. Providing choice in method, providing adaptability to the user’s pace are the guidelines for flexible design

Simple and Intuitive in use

When you see a CD Player, there are no texts in the buttons but somehow you know the way how to operate because of the icon. This is the best example of simple and intuitive use. User can think and understand what’s going on your system. 

Perceptible Information

Some information are hidden and some information are visible but can not be perceived like user can’t understand them and user can’t put them in the context to get the meaning out of it. Perceptible infromation means user can understand what’s going on your system 

Tollerance for Error

User can be from various community level. They take some time to understand your system, In that time, they may make errors.

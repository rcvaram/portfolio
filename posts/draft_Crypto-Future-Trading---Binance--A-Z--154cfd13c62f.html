Future Trade vs Spot Trade

You will get as you asked when you buy crypto in Spot Trade. Whatever you buy will be added to your assets. Hence whatever happens in the market will not affect your asset. If a loss happens it will be a paper loss which means the value will be reduced however when the value you increase you can sell and get the gain from the asset you had.

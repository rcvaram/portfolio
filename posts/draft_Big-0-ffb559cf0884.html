Big 0

“Big O notation is a mathematical notation that describes the limiting behavior of a function when the argument tends towards a particular value or infinity. It is a member of a family of notations invented by Paul Bachmann,[1] Edmund Landau,[2] and others, collectively called Bachmann–Landau notation or asymptotic notation.”

Did you get what is big O notation? You may get it I will explain a more simple way. Big O is used to describe the performance of the algorithm. It may be useful to find the algorithm is scalable or not. Depending on the data structure, the performance of the algorithm will be changed. For example, manipulating operations such as insert, delete will be more costly in an array other than a linked list. Why does it cost? You may read this article to find that.

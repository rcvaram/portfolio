Change My Phone In My Way I

In the first part, I will write how to install custom recovery in your phone. Custom recovery is important because for rooting or custom ROM installation needs the custom recovery. 

TWRP is a candy custom recovery software. This will help us to root the phone, backup the phone, flash the custom ROM and more.

First Step

Your boot loader should be unlocked and your battery should be more than 75 percentage. TWRP is build differently for each different devices you can get your suitable twrp for your phone from here.

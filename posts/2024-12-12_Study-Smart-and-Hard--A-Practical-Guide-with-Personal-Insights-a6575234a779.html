Study Smart and Hard: A Practical Guide with Personal Insights

Hitting rock bottom can be a solid starting point for a transformation; it's the perfect foundation to rebuild upon. From here, the only way is up, and the journey ahead is full of possibilities!

Studying effectively is both an art and a science. Over the years, I’ve discovered techniques that have significantly improved my learning process. This guide combines my personal experiences with practical steps to help you study smarter, not harder.

Step 1: Build the Big Picture

Before diving into a subject, create a foundational understanding or “big picture” of what you’re about to learn. Think of it as mapping the terrain before exploring. For instance, when studying Java 21’s virtual threads, I started by identifying key terms:

  • Threads and virtuality
  • Java’s existing thread mechanisms (platform threads, CompletableFutures)
  • JVM memory management (heap, thread stack, stack memory)

Once you identify these key terms, group them into categories. For example:

  1. Virtuality, JVM, and Memory Allocation: Concepts like heap, thread stack, and stack memory.
  2. Java’s Previous Thread Mechanisms: Platform threads, CompletableFutures, and traditional thread management.
  3. Virtual Threads Mechanism: How it improves upon older models.

Simplify these groups into an overarching concept: “Java’s thread mechanism has evolved to virtual threads, introducing a more efficient way for the JVM to manage threads compared to previous implementations.”

This approach gives you a structured starting point. Now, as you study, map new information to this big picture or fill in any gaps you find.

Big Picture = Simple Idea + Groups

Step 2: Dive into Depth

With your big picture in place, focus on understanding each group deeply. This step is where the bulk of your effort should go — about 60% of your study time.

For example, if you’re exploring JVM memory allocation:

  • Learn about the JVM’s architecture and its parts.
  • Understand how memory (heap, stack) is allocated and managed.
  • Investigate how threads interact with this memory.

Do this for all groups to develop a comprehensive understanding. As you study, aim to explain these concepts as if teaching someone else. This reinforces your learning and ensures clarity.

Depth = Effort + Resources + Curiosity + Existing Knowledge

Step 3: Add Specific Details

Once you’ve built a solid foundation, focus on the finer details. In the context of virtual threads, this might include:

  • Understanding methods like Executors.newVirtualThreadPerTaskExecutor().
  • Familiarize yourself with new terminologies introduced in Java 21.

Rather than memorizing these terms, connect them back to your big picture. Ask yourself:

  • How does this term fit into the model I’ve created?
  • Does it enhance my understanding of virtual threads?

If a term doesn’t map easily, highlight it as something to revisit. This process ensures you’re actively learning and reducing the “unknowns” in your model.

Specific Details = Connecting Terms to the Model

Step 4: Reflect and Visualize

After completing your study, revisit your model. Identify which parts are most significant and draw connections. You can create:

  • Mind maps
  • Diagrams
  • Flowcharts

These visual tools help solidify your understanding and make complex topics easier to recall.

If you find many terms still feel disconnected, revisit your big picture and refine it. A strong foundational model minimizes confusion and enhances retention.

Visualization = Reflection + Diagrams + Refinement

Mastering the Basics and Building Habits

The importance of mastering the basics, practising patience, and building habits cannot be overstated. Many overestimate their current skills, underestimate the time required to improve and get lost in endless “researching” without applying what they learn.

As the saying goes: “Ego is the anaesthesia that deadens the pain of stupidity.” Remove your ego, embrace the basics, and practice daily in small, consistent ways. Improvement happens incrementally and builds lasting habits.

By following this structured approach, you’ll transform your study habits, deepen your understanding, and achieve your learning goals effectively. So, pick a topic, start mapping, and build your way to mastery.

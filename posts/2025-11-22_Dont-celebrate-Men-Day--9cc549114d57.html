Dont celebrate Men Day…

When do you think people die?

When they are shot through the heart by the bullet of a pistol? No. 
When they are ravaged by an incurable disease? No.
When they drink a soup made from a poisonous mushroom!? No!

It’s when… they are forgotten

People don’t disappear only when their life ends. They start to fade when others stop remembering who they were and what they meant.

Mental stress and clinical depression do not choose a gender. They affect anyone because genetics, family history, financial pressure, trauma, and many other factors play a role. Even close friends or family may have no idea what someone is battling inside.

Feeling misunderstood, blaming others, or getting stuck in self-pity may feel natural in the moment, but it doesn’t move anyone forward.

Crypto Trading  —  Chapter I


Crypto is a digital asset. It was powered by blockchain. Bitcoin was created in 2008. bitcoin is kind of a mother coin however we have numerous crypto coins now in the market. 

Since Bitcoin is the mother coin, we say other coins as altcoins. The main reason why Bitcoin came into the picture is freedom. We need to middleman to ensure the transactions in a normal system but Bitcoin uses a decentralized system to ensure there is no middleman. 

Coin Vs Token

If something has its blockchain then it will be called a coin. For example, BNB has its blockchain called Binance Smart Chain. If something doesn't have its blockchain then it will be called a token. 

Crypto Terms


Total Supply

Total Supply refers to the number of coins or tokens that currently exist and are either in circulation or locked somehow. It is the sum of coins that were already mined minus the total of coins that were burned. 

Total Supply = The number of total coins in the market in both circulation and locked

Circulating Supply

Circulating supply refers to the number of coins that are publicly available and circulating in the market. The circulating supply of a coin can increase or decrease over time. 

Circulating Supply = The number of active coins in the market / publically available in the market.

Market Cap

Market capitalization is the total dollar value of coins that have been mined. It's the multiplication of the total number of coins * single coin at any given time.

Market Cap = Total number of coins * Dolar rate of Single coin

Trading Volume

The trading volume is the record of the purchase and sale of each coin. By showing how frequently the currency changes hands, its popularity and value can be ascertained. 

Limit Order

A limit order is an agreement to buy or sell an asset at a specific price. A stop limit order is an agreement to buy/sell at a specified price once the stop price is reached. 

Market Order

A market order is an instant buy or sell of a coin at the best available price.

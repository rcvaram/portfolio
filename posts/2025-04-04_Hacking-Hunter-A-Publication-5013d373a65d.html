Hey there, and welcome to Hacking Hunter — the place where developers, engineers, and tinkerers come to share bold ideas, clean code, real-world lessons, and the occasional “what-the-heck-was-that-bug” moment.

I’m excited to invite you into a space where your programming insights won’t be buried in red tape or ignored in an endless submission queue. Here, we value clarity, creativity, and most of all, the coder’s voice.

Whether you’re writing about backend architecture, AI experiments, debugging nightmares, clean code practices, or even your wildest side project — this is your arena.

💻 What Hacking Hunter Is All About

  • Fast publishing (no more waiting days for approval)
  • Zero fluff — just high-quality, developer-focused content
  • A space to grow your tech voice and personal brand
  • Weekly boosts to help your best work get seen

We’re here to celebrate developers who love writing, sharing, and shipping — no matter what stack you use.

🤝 How to Join as a Writer

  1. Follow the publication so you get updates and featured dev posts.
  2. Leave a comment below saying you’d like to join as a writer. I review requests daily and add new writers within 24–48 hours.

Need a break later? No problem — you can always return when you’re ready to write again.

🛠 What You Can Write About

We welcome all kinds of content from the software trenches:

  • 💡 Real-world coding lessons and war stories
  • 🧠 Deep dives into algorithms, architecture, and design patterns
  • 🧪 Explorations in AI, ML, LLMs, and experimental tech
  • 🐞 Debugging sagas that others can learn from
  • 🚀 Side project showcases and indie hacker journeys
  • 🧰 Frameworks, libraries, and tools you love or loathe
  • 🧵 Threads turned into full-length, valuable posts

Whether it’s frontend, backend, DevOps, data science, or something in between — if it’s thoughtful and developer-focused, we want it here.

✍️ Submission Guidelines

  • Word count: 200–2,000 words (flexible — just be valuable)
  • Original content only: Your ideas, your words. AI-assisted is okay, fully AI-generated isn’t.
  • Formatting: Use code blocks, headings, and spacing for clarity.
  • Images: Cover image required (Unsplash, Pexels, or AI-generated with credit is fine)
  • Tag your story: Add Hacking Hunter to your tags so it reaches the right readers.
  • Speed: Stories are reviewed and published within hours.

📢 Weekly Boosts

Each week, we’ll feature and boost selected stories for wider reach. This means your post won’t just be published and forgotten — it’ll keep working for you.

🧑‍💻 Ready to Share Your Voice?

If you’re tired of being lost in the noise and want a space where your code and ideas actually matter, Hacking Hunter is here for you.

Let’s build something powerful — a dev-driven community where we all grow by sharing what we’ve learned.

Follow. Join. Write. Hack.

Let’s make tech content great again.

The SQL-first approach with fine-grained control over data fetching is a clear advantage, reducing issues like N+1 queries and memory bloat. However, norm4j's simplicity might not be ideal for all projects, as it sacrifices the convenience of fully featured ORMs like JPA, which can manage complex relationships and domain models more seamlessly. It also introduces a learning curve for teams used to JPA's abstractions. In large-scale systems, norm4j could provide much-needed performance, but for smaller applications, sticking with JPA might be sufficient.

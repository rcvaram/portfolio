In my demo, I tried to dodge this by setting a default version in the Accept header (e.g., application/vnd.rcvaram.v1+json) if none is provided. So, old clients keep working on V1 until they opt into V2.

Still, you’ve got a point—clients might ignore headers entirely, and forcing them to adapt could spark a fire drill.

One fix could be making the version header mandatory (return a 400 if missing), but that might annoy users too.

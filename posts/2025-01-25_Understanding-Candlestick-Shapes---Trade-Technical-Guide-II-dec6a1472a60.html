The context is critical Candlestick Shape give more information when we check market context.

Candlestick Shapes

  1. Long Body Candles
  2. Short Body Candles
  3. Doji Candles
  4. Hammer Candle
  5. Shooting Star Candle
  6. Bullish Engulfing Candles
  7. Bearish Engulfing Candles

Long Body Candles — The candle has a long body

This communicates strong strength in sentiment

  • Wicks are small [High — Close \ Low — Open is little]
  • The body is big [Open — Close has a big difference]
  • Strong sentiment in the market
  • Big Green candle — Strong bullish in the market
  • Big Red Candle — Strong bearish in the market

Short Body Candles — The candle has a short body

This communicates less strength.

In this context, we are getting into candlesticks smaller and smaller. which predicts the red candlestick/ market can go down.

These small body candlesticks give much information when we see it in context. The above chart shows us the trend is going down.

Here the candlesticks are getting bigger and bigger. If you see this means the market getting strong.

The short-body candles are important when they take place in the context of a trending price.

Doji Candles

Open Price and Close Price are the same.

Doji candles can be of different types

  • Long-legged Doji — Both wicks are longer. This Doji communicates that there is a strong battle happening between buyers and sellers to make the price change but in the end, the market remains as it is.
  • Gravestone Doji — Open, Low & Close are the same price. only we have high.
  • Dragonfly Doji — Open, High & Close are the same price. only we have Low.

Hammer Candle

With a small body and large lower candle wick.

If it occurs at the bottom of the pullback will happen. as we know large lower wick means buyers are try to push the price above because of that the trend will reverse and move uptrend if it happens with the context.

Shooting star

Inverted of Hammer.

With a single candlestick, we can’t predict the market. So we need multiple candlesticks and also we need to understand the existing patterns of these multiple candlesticks. Let's see these patterns in the next post.

Image Brightness World

When I was new to computer vision, I don’t know how this brightness calculates in images. There are so many colour spaces and they hold the value brightness in channels like V channel in HSV, L channel in HSL, Y channel in YCrCb and L channel in LAB.

So really what is brightness?

Brightness is a perceptual attribute, it does not have a direct measure. Is it then how they calculate in colour spaces? There are some other measures

Perceived lightness is measured by some vision models such as CIELAB, where L* (Lstar) is a measure of perceptual lightness, and is non-linear to approximate the human vision non-linear response curve.

Luminance is a linear measure of light, spectrally weighted for normal vision but not adjusted for the non-linear perception of lightness.

Luma (Y´ prime) is a gamma encoded, the weighted signal used in some video encodings. It is not to be confused with linear luminance.

Gamma or transfer curve (TRC) is a curve that is often similar to the perceptual curve and is commonly applied to image data for storage or broadcast to reduce perceived noise and/or improve data utilization (and related reasons).

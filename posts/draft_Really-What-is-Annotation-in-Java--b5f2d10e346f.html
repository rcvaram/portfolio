What is Annotation?

Annotation is nothing but defining something(meta data) to a class/method or attribute to give readability for the class(without making comments) and also avoid runtime errors.

Here, we need to know there are two purposes for annotation.

  1. Readability 
  2. Avoid Runtime errors

How readability provides by annotation

When you see the annotation @RestContrlolller what will you think? you have some idea about that class. You knows that particualr class will act like a rest controller. Without comments, now you are using annotion to give readability to someone who looks your code.

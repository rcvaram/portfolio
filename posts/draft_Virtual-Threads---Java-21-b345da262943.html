sub routine [side-function ]

A subroutine is a code block that performs a specific task and can be called from other parts of a program. It simplifies complex tasks by breaking them down into smaller, more manageable code blocks.

coroutine [main — function]

A coroutine is a special type of routine that involves collaboration between multiple functions. Unlike a regular function, a coroutine doesn't have a single entry or exit point. Instead, you enter the function, perform some tasks, leave the function, and then resume where you left off. It's similar to having a conversation with someone on a walkie-talkie, where you take turns speaking and listening.

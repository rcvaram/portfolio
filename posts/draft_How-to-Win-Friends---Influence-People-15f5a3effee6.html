How to Win Friends & Influence People

 If you want to gather Honey, Don’t Kick Over the Believe

Criticism, condemnation, and complaints are futile as they put people on the defensive, affect their self-esteem, and cause resentment.

“Next time you feel the urge to criticize someone, pause and ask yourself, ‘What reasons could be behind this person’s actions or perspectives that I might not be aware of?’ Consider their circumstances, background, and the challenges they may be facing. Instead of jumping to conclusions, try understanding the context of their behaviour or decisions.”

Your choice of words can greatly influence how others perceive you. It’s important to consider the potential impact of criticism on others and their feelings.
